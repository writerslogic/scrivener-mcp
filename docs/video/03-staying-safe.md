# Video 3: Keeping your manuscript safe

**Audience:** cautious writers. **Length:** about 2:00.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A writer's hand hovering over a prompt, then pulling back | "Letting an AI near your manuscript can feel risky. Here is how Marginalia protects your work." |
| 0:15 | Scrivener open with the project; the AI app reports the project is open | "First, it will not write to a project while Scrivener has it open, because Scrivener would overwrite the changes. Close the project first." |
| 0:40 | Close the project in Scrivener, ask again, the edit succeeds | "Close it, ask again, and the edit goes through." |
| 0:55 | Scrivener's Snapshots panel with "Before AI edit" | "Second, before any risky edit it takes a snapshot. You will find it in Scrivener's own Snapshots panel." |
| 1:15 | Compare the snapshot to the current text, then Roll Back | "If you do not like a change, compare and roll back in two clicks. No special tools." |
| 1:30 | A scene with bold, italics, and a footnote before and after an edit | "Third, it edits the text in place and keeps your formatting, styles, footnotes, and images." |
| 1:45 | Scrivener's backup settings | "And keep your normal Scrivener backups on. Nothing replaces them." |
| 1:55 | End card | "Find the full guide in the description." |

**Accuracy notes for the editor:** the "project is open" check and the automatic "Before AI edit" snapshot are in the code (`ProjectWriter`). State exactly that and nothing broader. Do not claim the AI cannot make mistakes.
