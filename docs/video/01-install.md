# Video 1: Install in two minutes

**Audience:** a Scrivener writer who has never used a terminal. **Length:** about 2:00.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A chapter in Scrivener, then a chat window answering a question about it | "What if you could ask an AI about your whole manuscript, and have it work right inside your Scrivener project? Here is how to set that up in two minutes." |
| 0:15 | The Releases page, download button highlighted | "Download Scrivener MCP from the releases page. It is a free Mac app." |
| 0:25 | Open the disk image, drag the app to Applications | "Open the download and drag the app into your Applications folder." |
| 0:35 | Launch the app. The window lists the AI apps found on the Mac | "Open it. The app looks for AI apps on your Mac, such as Claude, BoltAI, or Raycast." |
| 0:50 | Click Connect on Claude Desktop. The pill turns green | "Click Connect next to the one you use. That is the whole setup." |
| 1:05 | The banner "Restart now" appears. Click it | "Your AI app needs to restart once. The app can do that for you." |
| 1:15 | Click Test connection. The green banner shows the tool count | "Press Test connection to check that everything works. A green message means you are ready." |
| 1:30 | In Claude, type the first prompt from the app's Try asking list | "Now ask something. Click any suggestion in the app to copy it, then paste it into your AI app." |
| 1:45 | The answer lists chapters with word counts | "There it is. Your manuscript, answered." |
| 1:55 | End card: link to the docs | "You need your own account with the AI app. Links are in the description." |

**Capture list:** download page, DMG window, app window in each state (not connected, connected), the restart banner, Test connection result, one real answer in Claude Desktop.

**B-roll for the end:** the same app list showing BoltAI and Raycast as connected, with the text "Works with the AI app you already use."

**Fallback segment (optional, 0:45):** "No app? Use the one-line installer" showing `docs/install-mac.md` and the Terminal command.
